Overview

Book Writer One respects your privacy and is committed to protecting your personal information. This policy explains how information is handled when you use the app and this support site. This app does NOT store user content permanently—all processing is temporary and for writing enhancement purposes.

✍️ Text Content – How Your Writing Is Processed

How your text is processed:

The only data processed is the text content that you choose to input. When you submit text:

  • Text transmission: Your text is sent to our secure backend to be processed by a large language model (LLM)
  • One-time use: The text is used solely for writing assistance and enhancement
  • Not stored: Text is NOT permanently saved, stored in databases, or retained after processing
  • No training: Your text is NOT used to train AI models without consent

🚫 User Content – What We Do NOT Store

We do NOT collect or store user content permanently.

Book Writer One does NOT collect, store, or retain:

  • Text content beyond processing
  • Personal writing data permanently
  • Any data that can be used without consent

The app processes only the text you provide—no user content is stored permanently.

✨ How Your Text Is Used

Your text is processed by an AI strictly to:

  • Analyze writing patterns to provide assistance
  • Provide writing enhancement for writing purposes
  • Produce suggestions and improvements for your book content

We do NOT use your text to:

  • Store user content permanently
  • Share data with third parties without consent
